How it works

Stridez adds a fast, playful movement challenge before selected apps open, making walking feel like progress instead of a chore.
1

Select your apps

Choose the distracting apps you want behind a walking challenge using Apple’s system app picker and set simple step targets that feel easy to repeat.

2

Walk and power up

When a locked app is opened, Stridez starts a quick step mission. Colorful rings fill, the mascot energizes, and progress updates live while you move.

3

Unlock and keep going

Once the movement goal is reached, your app unlocks briefly so you can continue, with the walk already turning a passive scroll impulse into active momentum.

Designed to make movement feel instantly rewarding

Stridez is not a hardcore fitness tracker. It is a lightweight behavioral product built around short bursts of walking, simple stats, and immediate visual payoff.

The experience combines step-based unlocking, live ring animation, daily streaks, mascot energy, colorful feedback, and low-friction social encouragement so walking feels fun enough to repeat multiple times a day.

Privacy and user control

Stridez is designed around user choice, transparent device permissions, and a limited use of Apple’s step and app-control frameworks.

User-selected apps only

Stridez only applies restrictions to apps explicitly selected by the user and does not take over device behavior outside those choices.

Movement data for the product experience

Step information is used to power unlock sessions, progress rings, streaks, and movement-based features inside the app experience.

Lightweight social interaction

Social features are limited to contracts, invites, and predefined reactions designed to keep participation simple, safe, and controlled.

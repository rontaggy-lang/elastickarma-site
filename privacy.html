Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ElasticKarma LLC ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in our apps, including ScrollScholar and Hydration Lock.

Information You Provide

You may provide content such as study notes, PDFs, images, or text when using the app. This content is used to generate features like flashcards, summaries, or learning challenges.

How We Use Information

  • To generate flashcards and study content using AI
  • To enable app features such as habit tracking, streaks, and progress
  • To improve performance, reliability, and user experience

AI and Data Processing

Some features (such as flashcard generation) require processing your content using artificial intelligence services. Your uploaded content may be securely transmitted to third-party providers to generate results.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the app:

  • OpenAI — for AI-powered content generation
  • Supabase — for backend infrastructure and data storage
  • RevenueCat — for managing subscriptions and purchases

These providers only process data as necessary to provide app functionality and are not permitted to sell your personal data.

Use of Screen Time / Family Controls APIs

Some apps use Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls APIs to allow users to voluntarily limit access to selected apps.

We do not access messages, browsing history, or content from other apps.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Your data is retained only as long as necessary to provide app functionality. You can remove your content within the app at any time.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Children

Our apps are not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

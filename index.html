Our Apps

We build products that combine practical utility with strong product taste. Our current apps in development are ScrollScholar, Hydration Lock, Stridez, and Still.

ScrollScholar

ScrollScholar helps students reduce distraction from social media while studying. Users can add their own study notes, generate AI-powered flashcards, and optionally require a short brain challenge before selected apps unlock.

Hydration Lock

Hydration Lock helps users build healthier habits by encouraging them to drink water before opening distracting apps.

Stridez

Stridez makes walking feel fun, immediate, and rewarding by requiring a short burst of steps before selected distracting apps unlock.

Still

Still helps users step away from distractions and enter a quiet space for prayer. By gently setting aside selected apps during a session, Still creates intentional moments of stillness, reflection, and presence without noise or pressure.
